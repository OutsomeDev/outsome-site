Curriculum Overview

An overview of the program and the deliverables.
*Setting KPIs (Key Performance Indicator)
Why Business KPIs are important? Who is the single customer ICP that our company is targeting ? As a startup CEO, you always have a lot of things to do, so it is important to prioritize your tasks (KPIs): · For what purpose · By what number · By when? This session will help you systematically set goals together, and take the steps to solve the problems you need to solve one after another, because you need to be driven by goals.
*ICP Definition (Ideal Customer Profile)
Why it's important to set up an initial ICP The key to going to market in the US comesdown to understanding the User Persona of your consumers. The key to entering the U.S. is to understand the User Persona of your consumers, which means you need to know who your target audience is and approach their problems, · Analyze the personas of your initial customers, · Watch what demands they are driven by, and · Write a story about how to sell and brand based on the personas.
GTM Masterclass (Go-To-Market)
Why Go-To-Market (GTM) is important All the KPIs and product planning in the world can only be successful if you're constantly communicating and selling to your customers. GTM is a methodology for how startups onboard their first 10 customers in the early days when they don't have any customers. In this session, we'll go beyond "where to engage" with real-world examples - where to engage, who to meet, what to talk to, and how to move on if it doesn't work.
US-Native IR Deck
Why Storytelling is Everything Your story is your most important motivator and source of energy. US investors want to know why you, the founder, want to solve this problem now. We'll cover : · Why here, why now? · Getting our one-liner right for investors · Preparing an IR Deck that works for us · Structuring your story and preparing an IR deck to help you fully localize your US launch .
